## Company

- What we do: international import and export. Focus: scrap metal export from the United States,
  aluminium and copper scrap, ferrous and non-ferrous metal trading, global sourcing, logistics coordination.
- Primary trade corridor: United States to India. We also work with counterparties in the Middle East,
  Europe and Asia depending on the material and market.
- We BUY scrap metal from U.S. suppliers (scrap yards, recyclers, processors, dealers) and sell it to
  overseas mills, processors and consumers.
- We work with suppliers of all sizes, from single-container transactions to recurring supply programs.
  What matters is a clear specification and a qualified counterparty.

## Materials
- Ferrous: shredded steel, heavy melting steel (HMS), plate and structural, busheling, cast.
- Non-ferrous: copper (including insulated wire grades), aluminium (extrusions, sheet and clippings,
  wheels and cast, mixed grades), brass, stainless.
- We source to the grade and chemistry a buyer specifies rather than trading from a fixed stock list.
  Other categories are taken on selectively.

## How a deal works (published process)
1. Requirement and specification: grade, quantity, packing, delivery terms, timeline.
2. Counterparty and terms: supplier vetted, samples/photos and certifications verified, price and Incoterms agreed.
3. Contract and compliance: contracts, trade documents, payment instruments; customs on both sides.
4. Shipment and delivery: freight booked and tracked, inspection at origin, follow-through to delivery.
